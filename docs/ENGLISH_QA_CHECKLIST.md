For every page, check: navigation, headings, body copy, buttons and links, form labels and validation messages, footer/disclaimer, image alt text, external-link URLs, language persistence after internal navigation, and English line wrapping at desktop and mobile widths.

The proposal preview pages and admin pages are intentionally excluded from this checklist.
